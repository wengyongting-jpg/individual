ONTARIO PREFERENCE — 2019 RULE, NO LONGER IN EFFECT
Under the 2019 edition, the Ontario-based supplier preference applied to all goods and services
purchases, regardless of dollar value, with no upper limit. This is different from the current
Provincial Trade Policy, which limits the preference to purchases below $121,200.

U.S.-BASED SUPPLIERS — 2019 RULE, NO LONGER IN EFFECT
The 2019 edition placed no additional review requirement on U.S.-based suppliers. This is different
from the current Provincial Trade Policy, which requires additional review or restriction for new
procurements involving U.S.-based suppliers.

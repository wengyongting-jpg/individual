SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — CALCULATING TOTAL PROCUREMENT VALUE (APPLIES TO GOODS AND SERVICES)
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

This rule applies to both goods purchases and consulting service engagements. It determines which
threshold tier a purchase falls into.

WHAT TO INCLUDE
When determining which threshold applies, staff must calculate the total procurement value, not
just the initial purchase price or engagement fee. This includes:
- the base purchase price or base engagement fee,
- any annual maintenance, support, or retainer fees associated with the goods or service, and
- the estimated value of related goods or services likely to be purchased from the same supplier or
  consultant over the reasonably foreseeable period (normally 12 months) of the relationship.

NO ARTIFICIAL DIVISION OF PURCHASES
A procurement may not be artificially divided into smaller purchases for the purpose of avoiding a
procurement threshold or any other obligation under this policy. Structuring a procurement, or a
series of related procurements, to avoid a threshold is a compliance violation and may be subject to
internal audit review.

SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — GOODS: PURCHASE ORDER DOCUMENTATION
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

This document explains the documentation requirements when creating and releasing a Purchase
Order (PO) for goods.

ATTACHING QUOTATIONS
When creating a Purchase Order, all supplier quotations that were received must be attached to the
Purchase Order record, regardless of whether that supplier was ultimately selected. This includes
any "no quotation" responses received from invited suppliers.

CONTRACTING AUTHORITY REVIEW
If a Purchase Order exceeds $75,000, it must be reviewed by the Contracting Authority before it is
released to the supplier. This threshold matches Tier 3 of the goods approval structure.

AFTER THE PURCHASE ORDER IS CREATED
The Purchase Order should be confirmed with the selected supplier before goods are shipped or work
begins. A copy of the finalized Purchase Order is sent to both the requesting department and Central
Procurement Services.

GENERAL DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
All procurement requests must include a documented business need. Staff should check that
sufficient budget is available in the relevant account before committing to a purchase.

SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — CONSULTING SERVICES: THRESHOLDS AND APPROVAL
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

This document sets out the three procurement value tiers for consulting service engagements at
SynthCorp, and who is authorized to approve an engagement at each tier.

TIER 1 — Below $75,000
Consulting engagements below $75,000 may follow a simplified process. A minimum of one (1)
supplier quotation is required. Approval is given by the requesting Department Manager. For
example, a $50,000 consulting engagement requires Department Manager approval.

TIER 2 — $75,000 to $121,199.99
Engagements in this range require a competitive process using the standard services Invitation to
Quote (ITQ) template, with a minimum of two (2) supplier quotations. Approval must be given by the
Director responsible for the relevant budget. For example, a $100,000 consulting engagement
requires two quotations and Director-level approval.

TIER 3 — $121,200 or more
An open competitive procurement process is required. Approval must be given by the Contracting
Authority.

These three tiers are the authoritative reference for consulting/services approval thresholds across
all SynthCorp procurement documents.

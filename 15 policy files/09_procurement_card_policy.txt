SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — PROCUREMENT CARD RULES
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

TRANSACTION LIMIT
The SynthCorp Procurement Card has a per-transaction limit of $5,000. Purchases above this limit
may not be made using the Procurement Card, even if the total procurement value of the underlying
purchase is below $25,000.

WHEN THE CARD MAY BE USED
A Procurement Card may be used for purchases below $25,000 (Tier 1 of the goods thresholds),
subject to the $5,000 per-transaction limit.

SAME OBLIGATIONS APPLY
Procurement Card purchases are subject to the same procurement policy obligations as any other
purchase, including confirming sufficient budget is available before purchasing and ensuring the
purchase is for legitimate SynthCorp business rather than personal use.

REVIEW
Cardholders must check that sufficient funding is available before using the card. Procurement Card
statements are reviewed monthly by the cardholder's Department Manager.

SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — THE APPROVED SUPPLIER PROGRAM

This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

WHAT IS AN APPROVED SUPPLIER
An "Approved Supplier" is a supplier that has been pre-qualified by Central Procurement Services
through a standing qualification process, rather than being individually vetted for each purchase.

HOW A SUPPLIER BECOMES APPROVED
A prospective supplier applies to Central Procurement Services through the SynthCorp Supplier
Portal, submitting business registration information, relevant compliance documentation, and at
least one business reference. Central Procurement Services reviews the application and, if the
supplier meets the qualification criteria, adds them to the Approved Supplier list. The list is
reviewed periodically and a supplier may be removed if it no longer meets the qualification criteria
or fails to comply with applicable procurement requirements.

EFFECT ON QUOTE REQUIREMENTS
Using an Approved Supplier may reduce, but does not eliminate, the need for additional competitive
quotations. The quote requirements set out in the goods and services threshold documents still apply
based on the total procurement value, regardless of whether the selected supplier is an Approved
Supplier.

Being an Approved Supplier does not guarantee any future purchasing volume from SynthCorp.

SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — CONSULTING SERVICES: TEMPLATES AND CONTRACT RECORDS
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

TEMPLATE SELECTION
The standard services Invitation to Quote (ITQ) template must be used for competitive consulting
procurements of $75,000 or more. A CFI-funded (Canada Foundation for Innovation-style research
infrastructure) project must instead use the designated CFI procurement template, to ensure
compliance with funder-specific procurement requirements, regardless of the dollar value involved.

CALCULATING ENGAGEMENT VALUE
As with goods, the total procurement value for a consulting engagement must include the base
engagement fee plus any related follow-on services reasonably expected from the same consultant
over the engagement period, including annual maintenance or retainer fees where applicable. See the
shared procurement value calculation document for the full rule.

RECORD-KEEPING
Service contracts, once signed, are maintained by the requesting department together with Central
Procurement Services, and must be retrievable for audit or review purposes throughout their term.

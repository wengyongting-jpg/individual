SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — CONSULTING SERVICES: NON-COMPETITIVE ENGAGEMENTS
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

This document explains the approval requirements for a non-competitive (sole-source) consulting
engagement.

BELOW $1,000,000
A non-competitive consulting engagement with a total value below $1,000,000 may be approved by
the Director responsible for the relevant budget, or by a Vice President, provided a Procurement
Policy Exemption Justification (PPEJ) is completed following the same PPEJ rules that apply to
non-competitive goods purchases (see the goods PPEJ document): the PPEJ must explain why
competition was not possible, and for engagements of $75,000 or more it must be reviewed and
approved by the Contracting Authority before committing to the consultant.

$1,000,000 OR MORE
A non-competitive consulting engagement of $1,000,000 or more requires additional, higher-level
approval from a Vice President or the Executive Procurement Committee before the engagement may
proceed, reflecting the increased risk associated with large, non-competitively awarded consulting
contracts.

The general limits on using an exception — it must never be used simply to avoid competition or to
favour a particular consultant — apply equally to services as they do to goods.

SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — PROVINCIAL TRADE POLICY AND SUPPLIER PREFERENCES

This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

ONTARIO PREFERENCE BELOW $121,200
For purchases below $121,200, SynthCorp procurement is subject to a regional preference under the
applicable provincial trade policy, favouring Ontario-based businesses where all else is equal, such
as price and quality being comparable between bidding suppliers.

RESTRICTIONS ON U.S.-BASED SUPPLIERS
New procurements involving suppliers based in the United States may be subject to additional review
or restriction, consistent with prevailing provincial trade guidance in effect at the time of purchase.
Staff should confirm current guidance with Central Procurement Services before committing to a
U.S.-based supplier.

SUPPLIER COMPLIANCE
Any supplier doing business with SynthCorp must comply with all applicable procurement
requirements referenced in this and related SynthCorp procurement documents. Non-compliance may
result in removal from the Approved Supplier list or exclusion from future opportunities.

CURRENT EDITION
This is the current, effective Provincial Trade Policy. It supersedes the 2019 edition of the
Provincial Trade Policy, which no longer applies and must not be used for procurement decisions.

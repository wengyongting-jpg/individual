SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — SUPPLIER FAIRNESS AND EQUAL ACCESS
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

EQUAL ACCESS AND FAIR TREATMENT
All qualified suppliers must have equal access to SynthCorp procurement opportunities and equal
access to relevant procurement information. Suppliers must be treated fairly and consistently
throughout the procurement process.

REASONABLE PARTICIPATION CONDITIONS
Any conditions imposed on supplier participation must be reasonable and must not be broader than
necessary to achieve a legitimate procurement objective.

FINDING OPPORTUNITIES
Open competitive procurement opportunities are published through the SynthCorp Supplier Portal.
Suppliers invited to quote should submit complete, written quotations by the stated deadline.

NO GUARANTEE OF FUTURE VOLUME
SynthCorp does not guarantee any future purchasing volume to a supplier as a result of past
business. Suppliers should direct questions about unclear procurement terms to the requesting
department or Central Procurement Services.

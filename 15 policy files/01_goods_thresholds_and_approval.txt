SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — GOODS: THRESHOLDS AND APPROVAL AUTHORITY
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

This document sets out the four procurement value tiers that apply to purchases of goods at
SynthCorp, and who is authorized to approve a purchase at each tier.

TIER 1 — Below $25,000
Purchases of goods with a total procurement value below $25,000 may be made using an informal
procurement process. No competitive quotations are formally required at this tier. A SynthCorp
Procurement Card may be used, subject to the card's per-transaction limit. Approval for an informal
goods purchase below $25,000 may be given by the requesting employee's Department Manager.

TIER 2 — $25,000 to $74,999.99
Purchases in this range require a competitive procurement process with a minimum of two (2)
supplier quotations. Approval must be given by the Department Head.

TIER 3 — $75,000 to $121,199.99
Purchases in this range require a competitive procurement process with a minimum of three (3)
supplier quotations, using the standard goods Invitation to Quote (ITQ) template. Approval must be
given by the Director responsible for the relevant budget, and the resulting Purchase Order must be
reviewed by the Contracting Authority before release.

TIER 4 — $121,200 or more
An open competitive procurement process (formal tender or Request for Proposal) is required.
Approval must be given by the Contracting Authority, in consultation with Central Procurement
Services.

These four tiers are the authoritative reference for goods approval thresholds across all SynthCorp
procurement documents.

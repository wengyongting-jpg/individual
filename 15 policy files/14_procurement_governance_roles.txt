This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

ROLE OF CENTRAL PROCUREMENT SERVICES
Central Procurement Services is responsible for maintaining and updating this procurement policy
and its associated procedures, and for overseeing compliance monitoring across all departments,
including reviewing the Approved Supplier list and administering the Supplier Portal.

WHAT PROCUREMENT PLANNING MUST DETERMINE
Before a procurement begins, the requesting department must determine:
- the total procurement value, calculated according to the shared value calculation rule (including
  related follow-on purchases over the reasonably foreseeable period),
- which threshold tier and approval level applies as a result,
- whether the need can be met through an existing Approved Supplier or an existing contract, and
- whether the purchase is being funded, in whole or in part, by a research grant, in which case the
  additional checks described in the research-funded purchases document also apply.

Planning this in advance prevents purchases from proceeding under the wrong threshold tier or
without the correct approval.

GIFT ACCEPTANCE GUIDANCE
Central Procurement Services guidance permits procurement participants to accept gifts or favours of
nominal value (under $50), such as a promotional item or a working lunch, from a current or
prospective supplier, provided the gift is disclosed to the employee's Department Manager. This
guidance has not been reconciled with the Conflicts of Interest in Procurement document, which
states that procurement participants must not accept gifts or favours from current or prospective
suppliers.

SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — GOODS: SUPPLIER QUOTES AND THE ITQ PROCESS
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

This document explains how many supplier quotations are required for goods purchases at each
threshold, and how the Invitation to Quote (ITQ) process works.

QUOTE REQUIREMENTS BY TIER
- Below $25,000: no formal quotations required, though seeking value for money is encouraged.
- $25,000 to $74,999.99: a minimum of two (2) supplier quotations must be obtained and documented.
- $75,000 to $121,199.99: a minimum of three (3) supplier quotations must be obtained, using the
  standard goods Invitation to Quote (ITQ) template.
- $121,200 or more: an open competitive tender or Request for Proposal process is required instead
  of a simple quote count; this is a formal, publicly posted process.

USING THE ITQ TEMPLATE
The standard goods ITQ template must be used whenever formal supplier quotations are solicited for
a purchase of $75,000 or more. The ITQ clearly states the goods required, the closing date for
quotations, and the evaluation criteria that will be used to select a supplier.

REQUESTING QUOTATIONS
Before supplier quotations are requested, a documented business need or procurement request must
first be recorded. If an invited supplier does not provide a quotation, that "no quotation" outcome
must still be retained in the procurement file as evidence that competition was genuinely sought.

SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — CONFLICTS OF INTEREST IN PROCUREMENT
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

WHO THIS APPLIES TO
Conflict-of-interest rules apply to all individuals involved in a procurement decision, including
anyone recommending, evaluating, or approving a supplier or consultant.

DISCLOSURE
Any employee involved in a procurement decision who becomes aware of a potential conflict of
interest must disclose it immediately to their Department Manager.

WHAT MUST BE AVOIDED
Procurement participants must avoid conflicts involving personal financial interests, close personal
relationships with a bidding supplier, or any other circumstance that could reasonably be seen to
compromise their objectivity.

CONFIDENTIALITY AND GIFTS
Procurement participants must keep supplier and bid information confidential, must not accept gifts
or favours from current or prospective suppliers, and must otherwise maintain professional integrity
throughout the procurement process.

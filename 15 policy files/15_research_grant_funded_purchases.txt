SYNTHCORP PROCUREMENT POLICY — RESEARCH GRANT-FUNDED PURCHASES
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE ONLY.
This document is fictional and was created for the PE6201 End-of-Course Project. It does not represent
the real policy of any real institution or organization. Any resemblance to real procurement rules is
coincidental and for demonstration purposes only.

NO EXEMPTION FROM STANDARD PROCUREMENT RULES
A purchase that is funded, in whole or in part, by a research grant is not exempt from any part of
this procurement policy. The same threshold tiers, quote requirements, approval levels, and PPEJ
rules described in the goods and services documents apply regardless of the funding source.

ADDITIONAL CHECKS FOR GRANT-FUNDED PURCHASES
Before committing to a grant-funded purchase, staff must check the specific terms of the research
grant, since some granting agencies impose their own restrictions on eligible suppliers, eligible
categories of spending, or required documentation, in addition to SynthCorp's own procurement
requirements. Any grant-specific restriction must be followed alongside, not instead of, this policy.

EQUIPMENT PURCHASES UNDER A GRANT
When a grant-funded purchase involves equipment, staff should also consider related ongoing
operating costs (such as maintenance, consumables, or service contracts) before committing to the
purchase, since these costs form part of the total procurement value calculation described in the
shared value calculation document.
